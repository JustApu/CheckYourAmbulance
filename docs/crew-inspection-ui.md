# Crew inspection screens

[issue #85](https://github.com/JustApu/CheckYourAmbulance-archive/issues/85). Non-normative implementation notes. The product rules are SPEC §6.1–§6.5, §7.3 (REC-1–REC-4), §7.4 (CON-2, CON-3, CON-6), §16.4 and §17.3, and the shell conventions in [application shell](application-shell.md). Code: `cya/inspections/views.py`, `api.py` and `templates/inspections/`; the row fields come from `cya/attention/` ([attention views](attention-views.md)).

## Pages

| Route | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `/inspection/` | Active vehicles with their operational status. Every built-in role (SPEC §3.3); the server enforces it like the other sections. |
| `/inspection/vehicles/{id}/` | A responsive vehicle-name/status heading, inspection-history and deficiency tasks, and explicit template-labelled start/join actions. The latest open session is shown with Continue or Join as appropriate; starting a separate inspection is secondary and confirmed. Active sessions and the signed-in user's own ended sessions remain visible. |
| `/inspection/vehicles/{id}/print/` | The paper checklist for outages (issue #9 Part A): the vehicle's current checklist with blank result, count or value, initials and time columns, and one seal-or-contents row per kit, for printing on US Letter. Linked from the vehicle page as "Paper checklist for outages". Same access as the vehicle page. |
| `/inspection/kit-reference/` | The kit reference: what a complete kit contains, per kit type, from each active checklist template's revision in force. Printed once per revision and laminated. Same access as the vehicle page. |
| `/inspection/sessions/{id}/` | The checklist screen. A participant only: a session the user did not join answers 404 exactly like one that does not exist. |

A vehicle in another organization, an inactive vehicle and a malformed id are all 404. A signed-out visitor is sent to sign-in and a user with no role gets 403, as for the other sections.

## Paper checklists for outages

The app is online-only, so an outage stops inspections in the app. Crews then use printed checklists kept in each rig (SPEC §17.8 DEP-11; owner decisions recorded on [issue #9](https://github.com/JustApu/CheckYourAmbulance/issues/9)).

- **The outage sheet.** `/inspection/vehicles/{id}/print/` lists the checklist in force for the vehicle when it is printed, grouped by compartment and vehicle system in the checklist screen's order. Each row has tick boxes for the item's allowed outcomes (Pass, Fail, Unable, N/A), a blank for a count or value where the item records one (with the expected quantity for quantity items), and blanks for initials and time. The header names the vehicle, each checklist template and revision, the version IDs and the instant the sheet reflects (UTC), plus blanks for the date and shift.
- **Kits on the outage sheet.** Each kit instance is one row, not its contents: tick "Seal intact" and copy the crew ID and earliest expiration written on the seal, or tick "No seal or broken: contents checked" after checking the kit against the kit reference, and note any exceptions. Kits do not start new pages.
- **The kit reference.** `/inspection/kit-reference/` lists what a complete kit contains (items and expected quantities) once per kit type, from each active template's revision in force, in three columns on US Letter. Print it once per checklist revision and laminate it; reprint when a revision changes. Each block names its template, revision and version ID so a laminated copy can be matched to the revision on the outage sheet. It is the agency standard: vehicle overrides are not applied. Until reusable kit definitions exist ([issue #129](https://github.com/JustApu/CheckYourAmbulance/issues/129)), a kit type is named by the label of the kit its checklist definition names, and the reference cannot yet mark items with expiration dates.
- **Paper size and pages.** Both print on US Letter. A long checklist continues on further pages rather than shrinking the type. Every printed page of the outage sheet repeats the vehicle and checklist version, so loose pages can be matched up. Page numbers ("Page N of M") come from the browser's print margin; Chromium-based browsers print them, other browsers may not.
- **No personal data.** Both pages are standalone, without the app header, so neither the page nor the printout carries the signed-in user's name. They show checklist definitions only: no check results, deficiencies or crew names.
- **Not a live record.** Printing records nothing in the app. If the checklist changes after printing, print new copies; the version ID identifies the checklist a sheet was printed from.
- **Failure.** If the checklist cannot be resolved, either page answers 503 and prints nothing rather than a partial document.
- **Sealable cabinets.** Not yet supported. The owner asked for compartments to be designated sealable or not, in the app and on paper; that needs a SPEC amendment and a schema change first.

**Outage procedure for the rig.**

1. Keep blank outage sheets for each vehicle, and the laminated kit reference, in the place your agency designates; the location varies by agency.
2. During an outage, record each check on the sheet: tick the outcome, write any count or value, and add your initials and the time. For each kit, confirm the seal or check the contents against the kit reference.
3. Keep completed sheets after the outage. Paper results are entered into the app once the downtime has ended, and the sheet can then be shredded. No separate review of the paper records is required.
4. Until the back-entry feature exists (issue #9 Part B, which needs its own SPEC amendment first), paper results are not entered as ordinary live checks (DEP-11). Keep the completed sheets until back-entry is available.
5. Reprint blank sheets, and the kit reference, whenever a checklist revision changes.

## Supervisor fleet readiness

`/fleet/readiness/` shows compact summaries of each active vehicle's explicit operational status, inspection progress and separate Failed, Overdue, Missed, shown high severity deficiency and suspension counts. The high severity summary uses the top two active, organization-configured severity ranks at the picture's as-of instant and displays their configured labels; each deficiency is ranked by the in-force revision of its level code at that instant (including retired levels), with its recorded revision's rank as fallback. If no levels are active, it counts all shown active deficiencies. The optional attention filter includes vehicles with non-In-Service status, failed/overdue/missed checks, active deficiencies at either of the top two configured severity ranks, suspensions, or a truncated deficiency list. These signals do not combine into a compliance or operational status. Each card expands to the existing obligations, deficiency and suspension details, including their truncation notices. Inspection, deficiency and status links open the current workflows; the deficiency summary link opens the oldest active deficiency, an `as_of` page remains a static historical picture, and the status link is explicitly labelled current. The live panel re-reads from the server and retains which vehicle detail sections were expanded.

`tools/browser_smoke.mjs` checks the readiness card layout, independent count labels, workflow links, attention filtering, keyboard disclosure, 48 px targets and horizontal overflow at 375, 600, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels and at a narrower 200% zoom approximation.

The landing page uses the configured checklist-template label. A participant continues their open session; a nonparticipant can join the latest open vehicle session using the existing command. If several sessions are open, each nonparticipant row explains that the primary action joins the latest one. When an open session is shown, a separate start is available under a disclosure and asks for confirmation before creating another session. A normal start can still join a session opened concurrently after page load; the command remains authoritative.

`tools/vehicle_landing_smoke.mjs` exercises the real vehicle-page script and CSS with synthetic sessions at 375, 600, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels. It checks the start/join distinction, confirmation and cancellation for a separate start, accessible task names, 48 px targets and horizontal overflow. `npm run test:browser-smoke` runs this alongside the checklist and shell smoke.

## Session endpoints

Authenticated same-origin JSON (CSRF protection stays on). The command layer authorizes, audits and replays by operation id; these views parse and map outcomes. Operation ids must be UUID v4. A refusal never echoes the command's text.

| Endpoint | Body | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/inspection-sessions` | `operation_id`, `vehicle_id`, `template_id`, optional `separate` (boolean) | `SESSION_OPENED`, `SESSION_JOINED` or `ALREADY_PARTICIPANT`, with `session_id`, `state` and `checklist_version_id` |
| `POST /api/v1/inspection-sessions/{id}/reopen` | `operation_id` | `SESSION_REOPENED` (a participant only, SPEC SES-3) |
| `POST /api/v1/inspection-sessions/{id}/end` | `operation_id`, optional `state` `CLOSED` or `INCOMPLETE` | `SESSION_ENDED` (a participant only) |

Rejections use the common shape: 400 `MALFORMED_REQUEST` (bad or unknown fields, a non-v4 id), 401, 403 `FORBIDDEN` (including a nonparticipant and an unknown session), 409 `SESSION_UNAVAILABLE` (the command refused: no such active template or vehicle, already open, not open) and the usual 503 codes.

## Checklist screen behavior

- **One tap, no Save button (REC-1).** Pass on a presence/condition or special-observation item sends the check at once. A quantity item takes a count (the server derives Passed or Failed from the target); a measurement item takes a value in the item's unit and sends Passed. A vehicle-reading item points to the odometer form (the server refuses it as a check).
- **Pending, Saved, Failed, Unconfirmed (REC-2, REC-4).** Pending is shown while the request is out. Saved appears only for a `COMMITTED` answer from the server. A definite server refusal is Failed with a plain-words reason. Anything else (the request failed, timed out, or the answer was not a definite one) is Unconfirmed: the write may or may not have committed. Retry resends the same request with the same operation id; a new tap is a new decision with a new id. The shell's indicator gained the `unconfirmed` state for this.
- **Scroll and keyboard focus after a check.** Rebuilding the checklist keeps the visible item at its viewport position. Focus moves to the item row while its action is disabled during a save, then returns to the action when the save result is rendered. The deficiency dialog keeps focus while it is open and restores it to the item when closed. If the item changed or disappeared, focus moves to the item or checklist list. Acknowledgement announcements continue through the existing status indicators.
- **Conflict dialog (CON-2, CON-3).** Each check carries `basis_check_id`, the newest effective check shown for that item (and kit instance). If the server answers `CHECK_CONFLICT`, the dialog names who recorded what and when and says nothing was saved from the tap. Cancel drops it. "Record mine too" sends a new operation id with `acknowledges_check_id`; if yet another check landed, the dialog appears again. The dialog keeps its row, so the proceed still goes out when a live reload has dropped the row from a Needs Attention Only list (for example because the other person's Pass satisfied the obligation); the basis and the acknowledgement are both fixed to the check the warning named, whether or not the row is still listed, so a newer check that a live reload has shown while the dialog was open cannot be skipped: the server answers with a conflict and the dialog appears again for that check. The outcome is said in the status line because the row is no longer there to show it. Whether to show that someone is viewing an item stays out (CON-6).
- **Live updates (SPEC §17.3).** The screen opens the vehicle socket. A push is only a hint: it triggers a reload of the checklist, and so does every (re)connect and the tab becoming visible, so a missed message cannot leave a wrong list. When the socket drops, a prominent banner says the screen may be out of date and the screen reconnects with backoff (1 s up to 30 s). The banner does not claim that unsaved items were not recorded: an item shown Unconfirmed may have committed, so it says to use that item's Retry (same operation id) and not a new tap. If the server closes the socket because the sign-in ended or access changed (close codes 4401 and 4403) the screen stops reconnecting and the banner says so and asks the person to sign in again. If a reload fails, a second banner says the list may be stale. The shell's own banner still covers the browser going offline.
- **Views and filter (VW-1, VW-2).** Normal and Priority and Needs Attention Only change the list at once and are saved through the audited preference endpoint, so they follow the crew member to other devices. A failed save only means the choice is not remembered; it never blocks the list.
- **Compartment navigation (issue #103).** A new inspection session starts at the compartment overview. An EMT can open a vehicle area/compartment, see its checklist items grouped by physical kit instance, and return to the overview at any time; All items keeps the flat list and existing filters. Each area summarizes checked items for the current window, due/overdue work, exception states and unresolved deficiencies. The selected view and visible item are stored in browser local storage under a key containing both the authenticated user ID and inspection session ID; malformed/stale keys fall back to the overview, and another user or a new session cannot reuse them. This navigation position is per browser/device; unlike the audited checklist view/filter preference, it is not synchronized to the user across devices.
- **Known deficiencies and status (VW-3).** Unresolved deficiencies show beside their item; the operational status and any restrictions are at the top.
- **Separate check result, window state, and deficiency lifecycle (issue #104; SPEC §7.5, §8).** The checklist labels the current inspection window separately from the latest recorded check, shows a deficiency's current lifecycle status and severity independently, and keeps Pass, Fail, Unable to inspect and measurement controls visually neutral. When a deficiency block already explains the attention state, the repeated Needs attention badge is omitted; the badge remains for attention without a listed deficiency. A Pass action never implies that a Pass was saved, that the window is complete, or that a deficiency was resolved; only the check acknowledgement, obligation state and deficiency lifecycle report those facts.
- **Kit instances.** An item in a kit is listed once per kit instance, labelled by instance, and each save names its `kit_id`. A save on one instance leaves the others pending.
- **Vehicle readings (ODO-1, ODO-3, ODO-4).** The compact form keeps mileage and optional engine hours separate. The first mileage entry selects miles or kilometres; later sessions display and submit the vehicle's established unit, which the server enforces. Each reading keeps its stored unit and historical corrections remain unchanged. An anomalous reading is refused by the server (`ANOMALY_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`); the screen then offers "the reading is correct", which is a new decision with a new operation id and `confirm_anomaly`. Changing the value starts a new operation. An unanswered request (including the confirmation) is kept whole, with its operation id, `confirm_anomaly` and explanation, until the server gives a definite answer; the form's submit and the confirmation button both resend it unchanged. Only one reading request is out at a time: its inputs and buttons are disabled until the server answers, so a late answer can never clear or replace a newer unanswered request. An ended session has no readings form, and the script skips the readings wiring so the checklist and socket still start.
- **Reopen.** An ended session shows a Reopen button instead of the controls. Reopening reloads the screen; completed checks keep their original times (SES-4). The other recorded participants are notified that someone reopened it ([notifications](notifications.md#which-events-notify-and-whom), issue #14).
- **Escaping.** Every server value is written with `textContent`, never as markup. The page config is emitted with `json_script`.

## Assumptions and boundaries

1. **Fail and Unable are implemented by [issue #86](https://github.com/JustApu/CheckYourAmbulance-archive/issues/86).** A mismatching count or an explicit Fail/Unable opens the [deficiency workflow](deficiency-workflows.md). The reporter selects an existing report or creates one with category, severity criteria, summary and description; Unable also requires a reason. The full request is retained for retry and conflict acknowledgement. History pages provide own corrections, administrator invalidation and supervisor completion over the existing audited commands.
2. **An odometer entry is not linked to the session.** `POST /api/v1/odometer-entries` still refuses `session_id` (`NOT_YET_SUPPORTED`), so the screen does not send it and an odometer save does not count as session activity (SPEC SES-2) until that is supported.
3. **Session changes are not pushed.** The session commands do not publish `session.changed`, so another viewer sees a join or an end on their next reload, not live. Check, deficiency and odometer changes are pushed.
4. **One checklist version per session.** A session is pinned to the version it opened with. The screen lists only rows of that version; if the vehicle's checklist changed afterwards it says so and asks for a new session.
5. **The basis is the newest effective check for the item and kit instance.** The server compares within the obligation's window, so the screen can show a conflict the server then does not raise, never the reverse.
6. **Saved goes with the row.** With Needs Attention Only on, a row leaves the list once its check satisfies the obligation, and its Saved indicator leaves with it. The acknowledgement was shown first, and the check stays in history; keeping the row visible until the next reload is a later refinement.
7. **No new front-end tooling.** Like the shell, the stylesheet and script are inline in the page (no static-file serving or build step, SPEC §17); a Content-Security-Policy would need a nonce or served files.

## Verification

Issue #103 browser smoke runs the real checklist script against synthetic API responses in Chromium at 375, 600, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels. It checks the initial overview, accurate mixed-status progress, distinct same-template kit instances, attention-filter exclusion of suspended checks, flat view, target size, no horizontal overflow, refresh restoration for the same user/session, and isolation for another user or new session. Issue #104 adds outcome/window/deficiency combinations, neutral actions and keyboard-accessible action names at those widths. These synthetic browser checks do not replace real-device or screen-reader validation, and do not prove cross-device navigation-state synchronization.

- `cya/inspections/tests/test_crew_ui.py` covers the session endpoints (open, join, replay, separate, malformed and non-v4 input, authentication, CSRF, method, participant-only reopen and end, refusals writing nothing), the pages (sign-in redirect, 403 without a role, 404 for unknown, inactive, malformed and other-organization ids, escaping, participant-only session page, config contents, saved preference), and the checklist fields the script records with (version id, newest check and author, and the conflict warning for a stale basis).
- The script was exercised in headless Firefox at 390 px against a stubbed server (no real network): a Pass, the conflict dialog and "Record mine too" (new operation id with `acknowledges_check_id`), an unconfirmed failure then Retry (same operation id and body), an odometer anomaly then confirmation (new operation id with `confirm_anomaly`), escaping of an item name containing markup, and the layout. After review, a conflict dialog left open while a live reload drops the row under Needs Attention Only, then "Record mine too", was run both under Node against a stub DOM and fetch and in headless Firefox against the stubbed server (the check is sent with the warned-about check as basis and acknowledgement, and the outcome appears in the status line); the terminal banner on close code 4401 was run under Node only. The second review's three cases were also run under Node against a stub DOM and fetch, each failing on the previous script and passing on this one: a newer check shown by a live reload while the conflict dialog is open (the proceed keeps the warned-about check as basis and acknowledgement and the server's new conflict reopens the dialog), an ended-session page with no odometer form (the checklist and socket still start), and a lost response on the anomaly confirmation (retried through the form or the button with the identical payload and operation id), and a second submit while the first odometer request is still out (refused, with the controls disabled, so the later request keeps its own operation id for its retry). **Not browser-verified:** a real server and WebSocket, reconnect after a dropped socket, the live-lost banner, quantity and measurement entry on a device, other browsers, a real phone, a screen reader and contrast tooling (the shell's accessibility target is not yet claimed), and glove or low-light use (M5, OQ-40).
- `cya/inspections/tests/vehicle_script_harness.js` runs the vehicle page's script under Node against a stub DOM and fetch ([issue #27](https://github.com/JustApu/CheckYourAmbulance/issues/27)). The session id from a start or join answer is percent-encoded and appended as exactly one path segment to the script's constant `/inspection/sessions/` path; the page config no longer carries a URL template, so nothing read from the page shapes the navigation, and `test_workflow_scripts.py` pins the constant to the server's route. The harness checks a UUID; ids containing `/`, `..`, `:`, `$` patterns, markup, `?` and `#` stay one segment that decodes back to the id; an empty, `.` or `..` id, or a non-string, never navigates and is handled as unconfirmed, so the retry reuses the operation id; and rejected and unconfirmed (failed or 5xx) answers do not navigate. It fails on the previous script. The Django wrapper in `test_workflow_scripts.py` skips it when Node is unavailable, as in the CI Python container; the workflow's `client-scripts` job runs it under Node ([issue #19](https://github.com/JustApu/CheckYourAmbulance/issues/19)).
