When the sheet is copied

There is nothing to submit: each record was saved when you tapped it. Keep the paper sheet until every record you copied from it shows Saved. An item marked Unconfirmed or Not saved is not saved yet: use its Retry button. Once every record is saved, the sheet may be shredded. No separate review is needed.

If the time or the people on this entry are wrong, ask a Supervisor or Administrator to invalidate it, then copy the sheet again as a new paper entry.
